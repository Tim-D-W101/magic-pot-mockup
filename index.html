About

Natasha's kitchen

The Magic Pot is owner-run by Natasha, and she's usually the one who welcomes you at the door. We cook from the ZCBF Clubhouse in Alton, where the industrial area's workers come for a proper lunch. Our curries and platters were voted Best Indian Food in the Zululand Observer's Best of Zululand 2024/25. Come for the mutton curry, the breyani and the bunny chow, and stay for the welcome.
